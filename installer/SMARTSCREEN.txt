ColorCorrect Studio v0.1.0 preview is unsigned.

Verify the installer SHA-256 and trusted source before continuing. SmartScreen
may warn. Do not treat a checksum as publisher identity or disable Windows security.

CPU LibTorch, .NET 10 and native runtime DLLs ship app-locally. Setup does not
replace per-user installed CPU/CUDA libraries. CPU is the default on all hardware.
Offline CPU sample workflows require no download.

Optional CUDA reuses or downloads official LibTorch 2.9.1/cu126 (~3.8 GB) into
the separate CCP-New/libtorch-cuda folder. Existing invalid folders are refused,
not deleted. Each worker loads one compatible LibTorch tree.

DirectML is bundled for supported Windows graphics drivers. CUDA/DirectML can
accelerate selected detector/LUT operations; NN training and many stages remain
CPU work. Check actual backend telemetry; policy selection alone is not evidence.

Silent CPU: /VERYSILENT /SUPPRESSMSGBOXES /NORESTART /LIBTORCH=cpu
Silent CUDA (explicit opt-in download): /SILENT /LIBTORCH=cuda
